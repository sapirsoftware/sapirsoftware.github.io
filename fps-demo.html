Try a capture here

This page is taller than your screen and has a bar that sticks to the top, which is exactly where other capture tools go wrong.

  1. Click the Full Page Screenshot icon in your toolbar.

  3. Then try Hide private info: drag over the highlighted name, email and card line and capture again. On this page that is free for everyone, as many times as you like.
